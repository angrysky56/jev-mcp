# Frozen baseline checklist

Read only the supplied evidence and candidates. Give one allowed primary label per case before viewing Jev output. Do not invent missing measurements or access permissions.

- Belief: Does the observation add support, counterevidence, repetition/irrelevance, or too little interpretable information? An unfamiliar idea is not disproven by a lack of data. Shared source roots cannot become independent corroboration.
- Question: Which available observation produces different predicted results under the hypotheses? Check explicit constraints and unknown prerequisites. Allow none; allow equally good alternatives.
- Adaptation: Is a possible outcome allowed to weaken the hypothesis? A promise to test or an admission of uncertainty does not answer that question. A specific, testable auxiliary explanation is allowed. Missing criteria are not automatically immunity to disconfirmation.

The initial baseline author also wrote the cases. These baseline answers are illustrative controls, not independent scientific measurements. To obtain independent baselines, export the case-only prompts and have a separate reviewer or future connected-agent session answer before opening gold labels or runs. No generative-model API is needed by this harness.
